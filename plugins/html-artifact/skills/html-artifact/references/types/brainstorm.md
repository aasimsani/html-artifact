# Brainstorm grid

For weighing N options side by side before committing to one, when each option needs its own writeup, mockup or pros and cons rather than a single comparison-table row.

## Spine

1. **Masthead.** The `h1` names the decision ("Pick the settings page layout"). The lede says what breaks the tie.
2. **Grid.** A `.variants` block (see `components.md`) with one `.variant` per option: a name, a short writeup or mockup, and a `pros`/`cons` list. Three or four options read best; past six, cut the weak ones first.
3. **Decision prompt.** One sentence above the grid naming what the reader should do: pick a winner, shortlist a few, or just react.
4. **Follow-up.** After the reader picks, a section (or a later message) that explains the pick and what happens next.

## Avoid

- More than four options with full mockups; thin the field first.
- Pros and cons that are adjectives ("flexible", "modern") instead of consequences.
- Skipping the decision prompt and leaving the reader to guess what "pick" means.
