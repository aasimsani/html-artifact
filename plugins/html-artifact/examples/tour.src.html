Rate-limit each API key with a token bucket in Redis.

Two customers caused 80% of throttled traffic last month, and today's only limit is a global 5,000 requests per second. A per-key bucket of 100 rps with a burst of 300 isolates them, and costs one Redis round trip per request.

07 · Test plan

Verify the limiter before enforcing.

Send 320 requests in one second with hey -n 320 -c 50 $API/ping.
Pass

Watch

Action
Wait one second and send 100 more.
Pass
All 100 succeed, because the bucket refilled.
Watch
429s here mean the refill is too slow.
Action
Stop the Redis replica in staging, then send traffic.
Pass
Requests keep succeeding under the global cap.
Watch
503s mean the limiter failed closed.

11 · Variants

Three ways to tell a customer they're throttled.

Header only

Retry-After: 2 and a bare 429.

  • Standard
  • Silent in most SDK logs

Header + JSON body

Adds {"error":"rate_limited","limit":100}.

  • Self-explaining
  • Clients must parse it

Email on sustained throttling

Emails the key owner after 10 minutes of 429s.

  • Reaches humans
  • Noisy during incidents
